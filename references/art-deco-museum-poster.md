# Art Deco museum poster

Use `SCHEME_ART_DECO_MUSEUM_POSTER` for armor, sculpture, historic objects, museum interiors, architecture, formal exhibitions, and subjects that benefit from ceremonial presentation without becoming fantasy imagery.

## Visual system

- Center one accurately observed artifact or architectural subject as the dominant object. Preserve its real proportions, construction, materials, silhouette, and identifying details.
- Use a restrained palette led by carbon black or charcoal, oxblood or dark brick red, antique gold or muted ochre, and warm ivory. A tiny secondary accent is optional.
- Frame the subject with simplified Art Deco architecture: monumental arches, stepped plinths, narrow columns, radial curves, or broad geometric floor bands. Keep these shapes large and quiet.
- Render the subject with precise antique-gold engraving or etched linework over dark fields. Let the surrounding architecture use flat screen-printed or lithographic color planes.
- Add subtle uncoated-paper grain, slight ink absorption, and small registration imperfections. Do not use glossy metallic rendering or cinematic shine.
- Use widely spaced geometric capitals and concise museum-style hierarchy. Required postal text remains exact and highly legible.

## Composition and restraint

- Maintain a clear hierarchy: artifact first, architectural frame second, title third, texture last.
- Keep ornament localized to historically plausible object detail or a few framing rules. Do not fill walls, borders, or shadows with decorative pattern.
- Use strong symmetry or near-symmetry, but allow one asymmetric red plane, window, or curve to keep the composition alive.
- Leave visible ivory margins or quiet dark fields around the central object.

## Boundaries

- Do not invent fantasy armor, ceremonial symbols, provenance, dates, collection names, or museum logos.
- Do not reproduce an existing exhibition poster, institutional identity, artist composition, or trademarked display system.
- Avoid generic luxury-advertising gloss, neon highlights, photorealistic 3D staging, dense gold filigree, wallpaper repetition, and competing decorative borders.
- Distinguish this scheme from `SCHEME_ORNATE_LINEWORK`: Art Deco museum poster uses a limited black-red-gold architectural color system and one monumental exhibition object; ornate linework remains primarily single-color archival engraving.
