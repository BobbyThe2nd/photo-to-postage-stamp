# Stamp schemes

Use the matching scheme text in the image-generation prompt. Keep the physical stamp requirements from `SKILL.md` in addition to this style description.

| Scheme ID | Use for | Prompt language |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `SCHEME_CHIN_PAPERCUT` | Chinese paper-cut window flower | Intricate traditional Chinese paper-cut window flower style; dramatic cutout effects, complex geometric lines, integrated auspicious patterns, and precisely hand-cut forms in a vibrant solid traditional-red palette. |
| `SCHEME_INK_WASH` | Ink wash painting | Traditional Chinese ink wash painting; misty rendering, delicate ink-tone variation, tranquil artistic atmosphere, monochromatic black, gray, and white with restrained light color washes. |
| `SCHEME_TRAV_VINT` | Retro travel poster | Whimsical flat-color mid-century travel-poster illustration; screen-print effect, bold color blocking, vintage paper texture, and gentle distressed detail. |
| `SCHEME_TECH_INTA` | Retro etching | Monochromatic fine-line intaglio engraving; vintage newspaper and scientific-illustration character with cross-hatching, stippling, and halftone dots. |
| `SCHEME_CHIN_WOOD` | Traditional Chinese print | Intricate traditional Chinese woodcut or print; detailed engraving, textured paper surface, rich saturated color, and strong edge definition. |
| `SCHEME_MINI_ICON` | Minimalist icon | Bauhaus-inspired minimal geometric composition; restrained mono- or duo-tone palette, bold geometric forms, thick clean lines, flat vector-like shapes, and a highly structured layout. |
| `SCHEME_SURR_POP` | Surreal pop art | Vibrant pop-art magazine-cutout collage; bold black outlines, high-contrast saturated colors, and graphic houndstooth or dot-pattern textures. |
