# Stamp schemes

Use the matching scheme text in the image-generation prompt. Keep the physical stamp requirements from `SKILL.md` in addition to this style description.

| Scheme ID | Use for | Prompt language |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `SCHEME_BLUE_PENCIL_FIELD_SKETCH` | Travel observations, boats, fish, tools, natural-history studies, and any subject needing an intimate handmade draft quality | Observational blue-pencil field sketch on clean warm-ivory drawing paper; pale graphite construction underneath, repeated searching blue-gray contours, partial erasures, slight overshoots, open edges, varied hand pressure, sparse directional hatching, and a few darker decisive lines that resolve the subject. Preserve source-identifying anatomy and silhouette while showing the act of looking and correcting. Use mostly warm paper, blue-gray pencil, and graphite with at most one tiny rust-red accent. Never render this as a one-click pencil filter, vector outline, technical blueprint, stippled texture, dense engraving, or uniformly finished illustration. Read [blue-pencil-field-sketch.md](blue-pencil-field-sketch.md). |
| `SCHEME_CALM_SOLID_WASH_DOODLE` | Everyday architecture, travel scenes, trees, interiors, objects, and calm observational subjects needing hand-drawn color | Calm solid-wash doodle on clean white or warm-ivory paper: direct water-based felt-tip contours, simplified opaque color planes, reserved paper-white gaps, and only a few broad translucent watercolor atmosphere fields. Organize the image through recognizable geometry, selective hard and lost edges, and one quiet accent rather than outlining every detail. Use hand-drawn marker lettering when text is needed. Never use ink-wash diffusion, stippling, granular blooms, mottled dots, scab-like pigment, vector-perfect contours, or a generic watercolor filter. Read [calm-solid-wash-doodle.md](calm-solid-wash-doodle.md). |
| `SCHEME_CHIN_PAPERCUT` | Chinese folk paper-cut with physically plausible continuous cut logic and narrative silhouettes | Traditional Chinese folk paper-cut in one vibrant solid red sheet, using solid silhouettes and open negative space as equal parts of the drawing. Choose a framed connected narrative, a supported freestanding emblem, or a limited hand-varied mirrored scene according to the subject. Preserve source identity and action through bold contour, linked gestures, broad paper bridges, and a few spacious cutouts shaped as slits, wedges, crescents, leaves, or holes. Keep one flat paper plane with no shadows or layered craft depth. Avoid dense filigree, pen-like internal lines, vector-perfect symmetry, identical repeated motifs, tiny lace, hairline bridges, etched shading, and cuts that could not physically survive. Read [chinese-papercut.md](chinese-papercut.md). |
| `SCHEME_INK_WASH` | Modern ink-and-color painting | Fresh modern Chinese ink-and-color abstraction organized through point, line, and plane: low-key cool-gray ink fields, decisive black structural masses, coarse broken dry-brush subject contours, translucent pale-gray washes, generous clean white paper, and a few jewel-bright color points. Use visibly weighted, resistant brushwork for the main subject rather than uniformly thin or willow-like lines. Preserve asymmetric negative space. Avoid dominant celadon or mint-green fields, generic misty landscape formulas, sepia, yellowed paper, antique patina, timid all-gray rendering, and old-fashioned souvenir aesthetics. Read [modern-ink-color.md](modern-ink-color.md). |
| `SCHEME_NAIVE_WATERCOLOR_BESTIARY` | Expressive animals, pets, and playful natural-history subjects | Naive watercolor bestiary on bright uncoated paper; one large, simplified animal with deliberately awkward, characterful proportions, transparent pigment blooms, uneven pooled edges, overlapping washes, reserved paper-white gaps, and sparse irregular black brush or pen marks for eyes, features, stripes, feathers, or fur. Use a restrained 3–5 color palette with one surprising accent. Preserve asymmetry, wobble, and handmade accidents without making the image dirty or distressed. Keep the background mostly paper white. Read [naive-watercolor-bestiary.md](naive-watercolor-bestiary.md). |
| `SCHEME_TRAV_VINT` | Retro travel poster | Whimsical flat-color mid-century travel-poster illustration; screen-print effect, bold color blocking, vintage paper texture, and gentle distressed detail. |
| `SCHEME_CLEAR_LINE_ADVENTURE` | Clear-line adventure comic | European clear-line adventure-comic illustration; precise, uniform black contours, flat bright colors, simplified expressive forms, clean white negative space, and playful visual storytelling. Use these broad graphic traits only; do not depict or recreate Tintin, Snowy, Hergé, or any existing character, logo, or copyrighted panel. |
| `SCHEME_CHIN_WOOD` | Hidden legacy: traditional Chinese print | Hidden legacy scheme; do not auto-select or show in a style selector unless the user explicitly requests it by name. Spacious traditional Chinese woodcut or print; 2–4 broad carved color areas, simplified contour cuts, textured paper, rich but restrained color, and strong edge definition. Avoid dense cross-hatching, fine engraving, stippling, and visual similarity to etching. |
| `SCHEME_MINI_ICON` | Minimalist icon | Bauhaus-inspired minimal geometric composition; restrained mono- or duo-tone palette, bold geometric forms, thick clean lines, flat vector-like shapes, and a highly structured layout. |
| `SCHEME_SURR_POP` | Surreal pop art | Controlled surreal pop-art collage; use a limited 3–4 color palette, bold black outlines, one graphic pattern at most, generous quiet areas, and one clear visual idea. Avoid crowded cutouts, rainbow palettes, competing patterns, and visual noise. |
| `SCHEME_VINTAGE_MIXED_MEDIA_COLLAGE` | Playful food, animals, everyday objects, expressive portraits, and subjects needing tactile energy and joyful imperfection | Fully redrawn vintage mixed-media collage with theatrical festival-poster tension. Build a continuous material texture ground from colored-pencil grain, chalk-pastel haze, wax-crayon drag, rubbed pigment, uneven screen-print ink, and sparse irregular white missed-ink flecks. Add separate localized pattern clusters made from hand-drawn dots, short dashes, small oval rings, circles, waves, scallops, and occasional individual spirals. Texture supplies tactile color variation; pattern supplies discrete visual rhythm and detail. Place pattern where it clarifies overlap, depth, structural change, light-dark transition, material identity, movement, or a focal detail. Reconstruct the source from essential identifying shapes; never retain photographic pixels or photographic surface detail. Avoid both empty modernist flatness and all-over decoration. Allow torn edges, imperfect registration, rubbed pigment, and visible overlaps. Never imitate a specific living artist or reproduce source-reference wording. Ban only dense, uniform, continuous, mechanically repeated or networked artificial motifs such as snake skin, fish-scale fields, Greek-key bands, worm or insect trails, cellular networks, maze filler, or pseudo-writing. Read [vintage-mixed-media-collage.md](vintage-mixed-media-collage.md). |
| `SCHEME_RED_PICTOGRAM` | Red pictogram | Restrained mid-century single-ink postal pictogram; brick-red ink on warm ivory paper, bold simplified silhouettes, crisp block geometry, minimal vintage print texture, and small production imperfections. |
| `SCHEME_ORNATE_LINEWORK` | Ornate linework | Elegant single-color line engraving on warm ivory paper; symmetrical ornamental borderwork, circular vignette medallions, delicate thin lines, carefully balanced typography, and archival postal-print character. |
| `SCHEME_ART_DECO_MUSEUM_POSTER` | Armor, sculpture, museum objects, historic interiors, formal exhibitions, and luxurious archival subjects | Formal Art Deco museum exhibition poster with one precisely observed central artifact, black or charcoal architectural space, oxblood-red planes, antique-gold linework, and warm-ivory paper margins. Combine simplified monumental arches and geometric framing with finely engraved object detail, restrained screen-print or lithographic grain, crisp hierarchy, and widely spaced display lettering. Preserve the artifact's real construction and proportions. Keep ornament subordinate to the object; avoid fantasy armor, excessive filigree, glossy photorealism, generic luxury branding, copied museum identities, or dense wallpaper pattern. Read [art-deco-museum-poster.md](art-deco-museum-poster.md). |
